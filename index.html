What can I track with ebbo?

Keep track of recurring payments such as streaming services, music, cloud storage, memberships, and more. Choose from the built-in catalog or add a subscription manually.

Do I need an account?

No. ebbo works without requiring an account, and your subscription data stays on your device.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. Track subscriptions in different currencies and view your totals in your preferred currency.

Is ebbo free?

Yes. ebbo is designed to be simple, free, and private.
